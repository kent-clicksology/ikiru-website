Services tailored to your holistic health

My therapy sessions are designed to support your whole being — addressing physical discomfort, emotional stress, and energetic imbalance. With over 30 years of experience, I combine a variety of healing techniques to create a personalised treatment plan just for you.

Shiatsu

Deeply relaxing hands-on therapy that releases held tension and restores the flow of energy through the body.

Cupping

A traditional technique using gentle suction to draw circulation into tight tissue and support the body's clearing processes.

Dry needling

Fine needles placed directly into tight bands of muscle to release trigger points and settle persistent pain.

Moxibustion

Warming herbs held near specific points, bringing gentle heat into cold or stagnant areas to stimulate healing.

Exercise therapy

Movement prescribed for your body and your week — building strength and mobility that holds between sessions.

Energy medicine

Working with the body's energetic system to clear what is blocked and settle what is overactive.

Food as medicine

Practical guidance on eating for your constitution, so the work we do in the room keeps working at home.
