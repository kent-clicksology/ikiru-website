My healing philosophy

For over 30 years my work has been grounded in one idea: that true health is not the absence of illness, but a balanced, vibrant state of body, mind and spirit.

01

Seeing the whole person

Every individual is unique, and health is a dynamic interplay between physical vitality, emotional wellbeing, energy flow and lifestyle. Rather than focusing solely on symptoms, I explore the root causes of imbalance. Healing happens best when the entire system is supported.

02

Integrating tradition and modern insight

I draw on time-honoured therapies like Shiatsu, cupping, moxibustion and dry needling, blending these with modern exercise therapy and energy medicine. That integration lets me address pain, stiffness and tension holistically — encouraging your body's natural ability to heal itself.

03

Nourishing from within

Health is deeply connected to what we eat. I incorporate food as medicine, recognising that wholesome, balanced nutrition provides a foundation for wellness. Food, combined with mindful movement and energy balance, is a powerful tool for transformation.

04

Partnership in healing

Healing is a collaborative journey. I work closely with each client to create care that fits their needs and their life. My role is to guide, support and empower you to reconnect with your body and restore its harmony.

05

Lasting wellbeing

My goal is to help you move beyond temporary relief toward wellbeing that holds. By working holistically we can tap into the body's innate wisdom and resilience, turning health challenges into openings for growth and renewal.
