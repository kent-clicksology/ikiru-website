About

Thirty years of listening to what the body is asking for.

I am a therapist specialising in holistic healing approaches that nurture both body and mind. My practice brings together a rich blend of traditional and contemporary therapies: Shiatsu, cupping, dry needling, moxibustion and exercise therapy.

I am passionate about energy medicine and the profound ways it supports the body's natural healing processes. Through gentle, targeted techniques, I work to restore balance, relieve pain and lift overall wellbeing.

Because health is so closely tied to the way we live, I also bring food as medicine principles into the work. Recognising the healing power of good nutrition helps clients make choices that support their bodies from the inside out.

Every approach is tailored to the individual, combining hands-on therapy with guidance on lifestyle and nutrition. Whether you are seeking relief from physical discomfort, carrying emotional stress, or simply want to feel at your best, I am here to support you.

How I work with you

Healing is a collaboration. Sessions begin with a proper conversation about your history, your symptoms and how you live day to day. From there we shape a plan that fits your body and your week, not a fixed protocol.

You are never rushed, and nothing is done without explanation. My role is to guide and support you as you reconnect with your body and find your way back to feeling well.

Services

Therapies that work together, not in isolation.

Sessions are designed to support your whole being — physical discomfort, emotional stress and energetic imbalance. Most treatments draw on two or three of the following, chosen for what your body needs on the day.

What I offer

  • Shiatsu therapy

    Deeply relaxing hands-on therapy using pressure along the body's energy pathways. It releases held tension, settles the nervous system and restores easy energy flow.

  • Cupping

    A traditional technique that lifts and decompresses tight tissue, drawing fresh circulation to the area and supporting the body's natural detoxification.

  • Dry needling

    Targeted treatment for muscular pain and tightness. Fine needles release trigger points that keep a muscle locked, restoring length and comfortable movement.

  • Moxibustion

    Gentle heat therapy applied to specific points to warm cold, stagnant areas of the body, stimulate healing and bring the system back into balance.

  • Exercise therapy

    Movement practices tailored to you, building the strength, flexibility and vitality that hold the results of hands-on treatment in place between sessions.

  • Energy medicine

    Subtle techniques to harmonise the body's energy system, easing the tension that sits underneath physical symptoms and supporting natural healing.

  • Food as medicine

    Practical guidance on nourishing your body through mindful eating and balanced nutrition, so the work we do in the room is supported at every meal.

Philosophy

True health is not the absence of illness.

For over thirty years my work has been grounded in a holistic view: health as a balanced, vibrant state of body, mind and spirit, rather than a list of symptoms kept quiet.

A whole-person philosophy

Five ideas this practice is built on.

Healing happens best when the whole system is supported and nurtured — not chased symptom by symptom.

  • 01

    Seeing the whole person

    Every individual is unique, and health is a dynamic interplay between physical vitality, emotional wellbeing, energy flow and lifestyle. Rather than focusing on symptoms alone, I look for the root causes of imbalance.

  • 02

    Integrating tradition and modern insight

    I draw on time-honoured therapies such as Shiatsu, cupping, moxibustion and dry needling, blending them with modern exercise therapy and energy medicine. That integration lets me address pain, stiffness and tension holistically, encouraging your body’s own ability to heal.

  • 03

    Nourishing from within

    Health is deeply connected to what we eat. Food as medicine recognises that wholesome, balanced nutrition provides the foundation for wellness. Combined with mindful movement and energy balance, it becomes a powerful tool for transformation.

  • 04

    Partnership in healing

    Healing is a collaborative journey. I work closely with each client to create care plans that fit their needs and their life. My role is to guide, support and empower you to reconnect with your body and restore harmony.

  • 05

    Lasting wellbeing

    The goal is to move beyond temporary relief toward lasting wellbeing. A holistic approach taps into the body’s innate wisdom and resilience, turning health challenges into openings for growth and renewal.
