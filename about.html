Meet Your Therapist

Over 30 years of experience in holistic, whole-person care.

With over 30 years of experience, I am a dedicated therapist specializing in holistic healing approaches that nurture both the body and mind. My practice integrates a rich blend of traditional and contemporary therapies including Shiatsu, cupping, dry needling, moxibustion, and exercise therapy.

I am passionate about energy medicine and the profound ways it supports the body's natural healing processes. Through gentle, targeted techniques, I aim to restore balance, relieve pain, and enhance overall wellbeing.

"Health is deeply connected to lifestyle."

Understanding that health is deeply connected to lifestyle, I also incorporate food as medicine principles into my work. By recognizing the healing power of nutrition, I help clients make choices that support their bodies from the inside out.

My holistic approach is tailored to meet individual needs, combining hands-on therapies with guidance on lifestyle and nutrition. Whether you seek relief from physical discomfort, emotional stress, or wish to optimize your health, I am here to support you on your healing journey.
